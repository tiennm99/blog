After going back and forth for a long time, I finally decided to open my wallet and spend on AI coding tools for both work and personal projects. And no, this is definitely not an April Fools' joke 😄

## Claude Max

I've been using Claude for quite a while now and even tried Pro early on. Claude models are genuinely great, but the pricing is pretty steep — so I mostly stuck to the free tier and spent a little on Chinese models or free alternatives. Recently, though, Claude Code has really blown up, and I can clearly feel that pairing Claude Code with Claude models outshines everything else by a wide margin. Luckily (or maybe not), two weeks ago Anthropic rolled out a 2x token promotion during off-peak hours. Even on the Pro plan I was still hitting limits left and right, so after thinking it over, I decided to go all in on Claude Max. Using it all day long without worrying about limits feels amazing 😂

Of course, I'll also be using Claude to help with blogging — hopefully my upcoming posts will be much better.

## ClaudeKit

Alongside Claude Max, I also bought [ClaudeKit](https://claudekit.cc/?ref=BWA910UK). This is a product by [Duy (Zuey)](https://substack.com/@goonnguyen), whom I've followed for a long time — I really enjoy his AI news updates as well as his practical tips for working with AI. ClaudeKit is a platform providing tools, prompt templates, workflows, and extensions to work with Claude more effectively. It's a passion project he's been pouring his heart into lately. I saw the big Lunar New Year sale he ran earlier, hesitated to spend the money, and ended up paying more now 🥹.

From my initial impressions, ClaudeKit is a prompting framework for Claude Code, similar to SuperPowers. However, the tools in this "ecosystem" fit together super smoothly and cover almost everything you need. It really feels like an "All-in-One" toolkit where ClaudeKit alone is enough. Hopefully ClaudeKit will help me get the absolute most out of Claude in my upcoming software development work. When I find the time, I'll write a proper review or share some tips on using it (though who knows when that'll be :3)

If you're interested, you can sign up via [my referral link](https://claudekit.cc/?ref=BWA910UK) to get **20% off** your first purchase!

## MiniMax Token Plan

Outside the Claude ecosystem, I'm also experimenting with the [**MiniMax Token Plan**](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/token-plan/intro). MiniMax is an AI platform whose models boast impressive long-context handling. Version M2.7 is even advertised as having self-learning and self-improving capabilities, which sounds pretty promising. Right now I'm hosting it on OpenClaw so a few teammates and my younger brother can connect to it as a fallback whenever their Claude Pro tokens run out. One senior teammate told me he'd rate MiniMax around a 7/10 compared to Claude — pretty solid potential! He even rated MiniMax higher than Z.ai's GLM-5 from earlier. By the time GLM-5.1 came out, my coding plan there was almost expired, so I didn't compare them much.

## Wrap-Up

My total monthly subscription bill has gone up significantly, but I see this as a worthwhile investment (or maybe because I hate wasting money, I'll grind even harder to get my money's worth 🤣). If these tools help me write code faster, ship fewer bugs, and save time debugging — then it's completely worth it.

I'll share more hands-on experiences after using this setup for a while. Stay tuned!
