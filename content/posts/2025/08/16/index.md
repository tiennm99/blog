Hey everyone! Today I'd like to introduce a little side project I just finished — **VNGeoGuessr**, a location-guessing game (like GeoGuessr) built specifically for Vietnam.

## What is VNGeoGuessr?

VNGeoGuessr is a clone of GeoGuessr (though obviously much jankier :v), designed specifically around cities in Vietnam. Instead of having to guess unfamiliar places all over the world, you can now challenge yourself with the streets of our own homeland, Vietnam. Right now, VNGeoGuessr supports major cities like Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang, Da Lat, and Duc Hoa (my hometown :v). Feel free to send suggestions so I can add more places :v

## How to Play

The game is super simple:
1. The system displays a random Street View panorama from the city you selected
2. Look around and guess where that spot is on the map
3. The closer your guess is to the actual location, the higher your score
4. Challenge yourself and get your name onto the leaderboard :v

## Highlights

### Dual Leaderboards
VNGeoGuessr features two types of leaderboards to keep things competitive:
- **Score Leaderboard**: Slow and steady wins the race — even if your guesses aren't great, playing long enough can still get you to the top :v
- **Minimum Distance Leaderboard**: Built for hardcore players aiming for pinpoint accuracy on every single guess :v

### Local Data
Locations in the game are pulled from major cities across Vietnam, making them much more familiar and relatable for Vietnamese players.
