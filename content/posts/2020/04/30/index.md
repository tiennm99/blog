---
title: "Making the Login Screen Mouse Cursor Look Better"
date: 2020-04-30
tags: [ "Arch", "Linux", "Cursor" ]
---

As the title suggests, to change the mouse cursor on your login screen, edit the **Inherits** value in `/usr/share/icons/default/index.theme`.

Typically, if you want the login screen cursor to match your current desktop cursor, you can check `~/.icons/default/index.theme` to see the theme name to use!

Good luck!
